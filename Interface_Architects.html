Executed projects

2015 to 2024  |  Lusaka, Zambia

Upgrade of the Kenneth Kaunda International Airport (KKIA)

In 2015, the Government of the Republic of Zambia began a three-year, US$360 million expansion and improvement of the airport, funded by a loan from the Exim Bank of China.

The work included a new two-storey terminal building with 22 check-in counters, a presidential terminal, a new air traffic control building and tower, a hotel, a fire station, a shopping mall, an office park, works at ZASTI and a crash-aid hospital.

Construction was substantially completed by late January 2020. The new terminal opened on 5 August 2021. International flights use the new Terminal Two; domestic flights use the original Terminal One.

2012 to 2021  |  Between Zambia and Botswana

The Kazungula Bridge and One Stop Border Post

The African Development Bank Group supported the project with an African Development Fund loan of $76.5 million to the Government of Zambia.

Alongside funding from the two governments, the project was co-financed by the Japan International Cooperation Agency and the EU-Africa Infrastructure Trust Fund. Total cost was $259.3 million. It was approved in December 2011 and completed in December 2020.

2000 to 2005  |  Chirundu, between Zambia and Zimbabwe

The Chirundu Bridge and One Stop Border Post

The new Chirundu Bridge was built by the Kajima Corporation from 2000 to 2002 and opened on 12 December 2002. It stands 90 metres upstream from the Otto Beit Bridge, using the same approaches and border control area. It is a two-lane, three-span continuous prestressed concrete box girder bridge, 400 metres long and 10.3 metres wide.

Pre-contract services began around August 1999, with Nippon Koei Co. Ltd as project engineering consultants and Chodai Co. Ltd as bridge specialists. As their counterpart contribution, the governments of Zambia and Zimbabwe upgraded their border facilities for smoother traffic flow and faster processing of cross-border documents, with a view to converting the border to a One Stop Border Facility.
